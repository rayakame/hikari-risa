Ready-made hooks in `risa.prefab`: the checks `owner_only`, `invoker_only`, `has_permissions`, `bot_has_permissions`, `has_roles` and `expires_after`, the cooldowns `fixed_window` and `sliding_window`, and `max_concurrency`. Each turns a click away with an exception derived from `ClickRejectedError`. Cooldowns and `max_concurrency` count in memory, or in one of the client's stores with `store=` and `key=`.
