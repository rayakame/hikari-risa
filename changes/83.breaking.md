Error handlers take the click's `ExecutionPipeline` and its `ComponentContext`, as `async def on_error(pipeline, ctx) -> bool`, and `ClickFailure` is gone. `pipeline.failures` lists every failure of the click in order, `pipeline.find(SomeError)` returns the first of a type, and `FailureStage` moved to `risa.hooks` and gained `HOOK`. A click no error handler takes is logged as one entry with an `ExceptionGroup`.
